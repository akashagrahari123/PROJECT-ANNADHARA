Donation Standards

  • Fresh & Unserved: Only untouched buffet and kitchen reserve food is accepted.
  • Temperature Control: Hot items kept >60°C; dairy & salads chilled <5°C.
  • Hygienic Packing: Packed in clean covered trays, buckets, or food-grade boxes.
  • Rapid Collection: Shelters collect or volunteers dispatch within 60–90 mins.
