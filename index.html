Core Mission

Why Annadhara Exists

Dedicated to creating an equitable food ecosystem where no stomach stays empty and no edible meal reaches a landfill.

Share Surplus Food

We transform excess food from weddings, banquets, and restaurants into dignified, nutritious meals for those facing acute economic distress.

Nourish Vulnerable Lives

Children in orphanages, elders in care sanctuaries, and daily wage workers receive wholesome nourishment that sustains health and hope.

Protect the Planet

Decomposing food in landfills produces methane, a potent greenhouse gas. Rescuing meals directly combats global climate change.
